Part 1: Making a picture in Photopea

Every page picture is a 1600 × 1200 pixel image.

File → New. Type 1600 for width and 1200 for height, with the units on Pixels, then click Create.
File → Open & Place and pick your landscape. Hold Shift and drag a corner until it fills the whole canvas with no checkerboard showing. Press Enter.
File → Open & Place again and pick your character PNG. Shift-drag a corner to resize it, drag it where you want it, and press Enter.
Fix the handles if they vanish. Click the layer in the Layers panel, then press Ctrl+T (Cmd+T on Mac). Untick Auto-Select at the top so you don't grab the wrong layer.
Zoom out with Ctrl+0 to check there's no checkerboard left and nothing important sits at the edges.
File → Export As → JPG, quality 80-85%. Name it 1.jpg, 2.jpg and so on, and cover.jpg for the cover.

Optional shelf picture: make a tall picture at 800 × 1000 and name it thumb.jpg. If you skip it, the shelf uses your cover.

Part 2: Adding a book
Open storybooks/books/ and make a new folder with a lowercase name and no spaces, like the-sleepy-bear.
Put your exported pictures in that folder.
Copy book.json from another book into the new folder.
Open that book.json and change the title, the author, and each page's picture name and story text.
Open storybooks/library.json and add the folder name to the list: ["the-lost-kite", "the-sleepy-bear"]
Commit to GitHub. Wait a minute or two, then hard refresh the site (Ctrl+Shift+R).

Page order in a book follows the order of the pages in its book.json. Book order on the shelf follows the order of names in library.json.

Part 3: Removing a book
Open library.json and delete the book's name from the list.
Delete the book's folder from storybooks/books/.
Commit.
